Close a council. Only the chair (parent that spawned the council) can call this.

Use this when:
- You have everything you need from the council (all the answers, enough findings to synthesize) and don't want to wait for any straggling members.
- A council member is stuck and you've decided to wrap up rather than wait.
- The work has changed direction and the council is no longer useful.

Once closed, all running member loops are cancelled. Their session histories remain on disk and can still be inspected via the table — closing only stops new activity. The chair (you) then synthesizes the final answer from the table the parent has been seeing.

Parameters:
- `council_id` (optional): which council to close. Default: your most recently spawned council.
- `reason` (optional): short reason recorded in the bus event for observability.
