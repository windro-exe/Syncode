Read the council "table" — every entry posted by any member or the chair. Use this when you want to see what other members are working on, what answers they've produced, or what's been asked.

If you're a council member, this defaults to YOUR council. The chair (the parent that spawned the council) can pass `council_id` explicitly to view any of their councils.

Parameters:
- `council_id` (optional): which council to view. Default: the council you belong to (members) or your most recently spawned council (chair).
- `since` (optional): only return entries with index > this. Use to fetch deltas since the last time you viewed.
- `limit` (optional, default 30): max entries to return.
- `mine` (optional): if true, filter to entries broadcast to all + addressed to your role + your own posts. Useful when the council is busy.

Returns a structured listing of entries with kind, sender, timestamp, and content. Also reports member statuses (thinking / working / done / stuck) so you know who's still active.
